# Open Design Questions

This page tracks design problems that are known but not yet resolved. Unlike an [ADR](records.md), nothing listed here is a decision — these are backlog items for discussion. Once a question is resolved, write it up as a numbered ADR in the [Records](records.md), then update or remove its entry below.
