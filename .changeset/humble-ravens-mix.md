We've updated the way we present the match results (of the submitted query / filter). Before we used to show it on hover of the row (which was kind of a nuisance from a UX-perspective), now we've added a dedicated column for it showing the score of the match, and we show which fields are matched on in a popover (which you'll see when hovering over the "score" cell).
